About

Cat plushies are decorative plushies inspired by the various cat↗ mob variants!

Obtaining

Crafting

Recipes

To craft an ocelot plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two green wool.
  • Opposite corners: Place two brown wool.
  • Remaining slots: Fill all remaining slots with yellow wool.

To craft a white cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with white wool.
  • Middle row: Place yellow wool, any raw fish, and light blue wool.

To craft a tabby cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with brown wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two orange wool blocks.

To craft a tuxedo cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with black wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two green wool.

To craft a red cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with orange wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two green wool.

To craft a siamese cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top row: Fill slots with brown wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two black wool.
  • Bottom row: Fill slots with white wool.

To craft a british shorthair cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with light gray wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two yellow wool.

To craft a calico cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top row: Place black wool, white wool, and orange wool.
  • Middle row: Place yellow wool, any raw fish, and light blue wool.
  • Bottom row: Fill slots with white wool.

To craft a persian cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with yellow wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two black wool.

To craft a ragdoll cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Place light gray wool, white wool, light gray wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two light blue wool.
  • Bottom row: Fill slots with white wool.

To craft a black cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with black wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two orange wool.

To craft a jellie cat plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top row: Fill slots with gray wool.
  • Middle row: Place any raw fish between two black wool.
  • Bottom row: Fill slots with white wool.

Usage

Interaction

A cat plushie can alternate between sitting and standing by using↗ it. To place a block against the plushie instead, you must sneak↗.

Technical

Configuration

Toggles the availability of cat plushies:

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Cat Plushies
  • Config File Key: enable_cat_plushies
    • true / Yes: Enabled (Default)
    • false / No: Disabled and hidden
