About

The neutral plushies are decorative blocks that are inspired by Minecraft's various neutral mobs.

Placement

When placing a plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!

Enderman Plushie

Enderman Plushie

The enderman plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the enderman↗ mob. The plushie’s grass block changes color to match its current biome.

Crafting Recipe

To craft an enderman plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top row: Fill slots with black wool.
  • Middle row: Place an ender pearl between two magenta wool.
  • Bottom row: Place black wool, grass block, and black wool.
