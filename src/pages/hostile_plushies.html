About

The hostile plushies are decorative blocks that are inspired by Minecraft's various hostile mobs.

Placement

When placing a plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!

Hoglin Plushies

The hoglin plushie and zoglin plushie are decorative plushies inspired by the hoglin↗ and zoglin↗ mob.

Crafting Recipe

To craft a hoglin plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with terracotta.
  • Middle row: Place a porkchop between two white wool.

To craft a zoglin plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top row: Fill slots with white wool.
  • Middle row: Place a porkchop between two black wool.
  • Bottom row: Fill slots with pink wool.

Vex Plushie

The vex plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the vex↗ mob. The plushie emits a light level of 10 when placed.

Crafting Recipe

To craft a vex plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with light gray wool.
  • Middle row: Place an emerald between two white wool.
