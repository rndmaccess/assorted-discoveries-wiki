Allay Plushie

About

The allay plushie is a plushie inspired by the allay↗ mob. The plushie emits a light level of 10 when placed.

Obtaining

Crafting

To craft an allay plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with light blue wool.
  • Middle row: Place an amethyst shard between two white wool.
