About

The wolf plushies are decorative plushies inspired by the various wolf↗ mob variants!

Obtaining

Crafting

Recipes

To craft a pale wolf plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with white wool.
  • Middle row: Place a bone between two black wool.

To craft an ashen wolf plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top row: Fill slots with light gray wool.
  • Middle row: Place a bone between two black wool.
  • Bottom row: Fill slots with white wool.

To craft a black wolf plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with black wool.
  • Middle row: Place a bone between two gray wool.

To craft a chestnut wolf plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top & bottom rows: Fill slots with brown wool.
  • Middle row: Place a bone between two black wool.

To craft a rusty wolf plushie, place the items in a crafting table as follows:

  • Top row: Fill slots with brown wool.
  • Middle row: Place a bone between two black wool.
  • Top row: Fill slots with white wool.

Usage

Interaction

A wolf plushie can alternate between sitting and standing by using↗ it. To place a block against the plushie instead, you must sneak↗.

Technical

Configuration

Toggles the availability of wolf plushies:

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Wolf Plushies
  • Config File Key: enable_wolf_plushies
    • true / Yes: Enabled (Default)
    • false / No: Disabled and hidden
