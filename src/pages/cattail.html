Decorative Blocks Farming & Food

About

Cattails are two-block-high plants that generate on the banks of rivers and within swamp and mangrove swamp biomes.

Placement

  • Cattails can be placed:
    • On a block directly adjacent to water.
    • Inside a single layer of water (as a waterlogged block).
  • They cannot be placed in water deeper than one block.

Generation

Cattails generate naturally in swamps and on the shores of warm rivers, appearing on blocks bordering water or in shallow water.

For a list of tags used in cattail generation, see the tags section.

Usage

Potting

Cattails can be placed in flower pots.

Composting

Placing cattails into a composter has a 50% chance of raising the compost level by one.

Bone Meal

Using bone meal on either the top or bottom half causes it to drops an additional cattail item.

Technical

Tags

Cattails generate in biomes included in the following tags:

  • c:is_river - Only if the biome's temperature is greater than or equal to 0.15 (warm enough to rain).
  • c:is_swamp - Generates in any included swamp biome.
Configuration

Toggles the world generation and availability of cattails.

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Cattails
  • Config File Key: enable_cattails
    • true / Yes: Enabled (Default)
    • false / No: Disabled and hidden
